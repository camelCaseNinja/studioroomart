Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the StudioRoomArt app, which the shop's owner uses to read public trend data from the Pinterest API for the shop's own product research.

Who uses the app

Only the shop's owner. The app has no other users and offers no sign-up.

What the app reads

Trending search keywords and their search-volume trends, which Pinterest provides in aggregate. These contain no information about individual people.

What the app stores

The access token from the owner's own Pinterest login, kept encrypted so the app can make its read-only requests. Trend keywords are kept as part of the shop's private weekly research notes. The app collects no personal data about anyone else.

What the app shares

Nothing. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest's settings, and the stored token is then deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a message to the StudioRoomArt shop on Etsy.
